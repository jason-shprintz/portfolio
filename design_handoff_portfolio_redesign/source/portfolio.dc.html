01 — About

Where it started

My passion for programming was sparked when I was in high school. I created a game called “Perfect Pump” on the TI-83+ calculator. The game had a number displayed in currency, and the goal was to press a button and stop it perfectly on $20. The game was simple, but everyone really enjoyed it, and I vividly remember loving the feeling of making a program that everyone else used.
